# Code of Conduct

TickPuff is meant to be a calm, friendly project, and we want working on it to feel the same way.

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age,
body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression,
level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be welcoming and patient, especially with people who are new to open source.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Respect people's time, privacy and boundaries.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments, in public or in private.
- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their explicit permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained off-topic noise.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — issues, pull requests, discussions and any other
channel run by the project — and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the maintainers privately through the
contact options on the maintainers' GitHub profiles (for example
[@WiseHax](https://github.com/WiseHax)), rather than in a public thread. Reports will be reviewed promptly and
handled confidentially.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't
follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior they find harmful.
Responses scale with severity: a private warning, a public warning, a temporary ban, or a permanent ban.
