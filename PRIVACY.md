# TickPuff privacy policy

_Last updated: October 10, 2026_

TickPuff is a desktop clock with a living world and a companion. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no
tracking.

## What stays on your computer

Everything you enter or choose — settings, tasks, notes, focus history, your weather location — is stored only on
your computer, in the app's local storage. It is never uploaded anywhere.

## When TickPuff goes online

TickPuff only connects to the internet in these cases:

- **Weather (optional).** If you set a weather location, TickPuff asks [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com/) for
  that location's forecast. Only the coordinates of the place you chose are sent. Open-Meteo's own terms and
  privacy policy apply to that request. The location is also used, on your computer only, to work out sunrise,
  sunset and the season.
- **City search (optional).** When you search for a city in the weather settings, the text you type is sent to
  Open-Meteo's geocoding service to find matching places.
- **Update check (GitHub download only).** The version downloaded from GitHub checks GitHub once at startup for
  a newer release. You can turn this off in Settings → About. The Microsoft Store version doesn't do this; the
  Store handles updates.
- **Links.** When you open a link (such as the source code), it opens in your web browser.

## Information TickPuff reads on your computer

Some optional widgets read information locally to display it. None of it leaves your computer:

- **System monitor:** CPU, memory and GPU usage.
- **Now playing:** the title and artist of the media currently playing in Windows (also used so the companion
  can dance to music).
- **AI workspace:** whether Antigravity or Claude Code are installed or running, by checking process names and
  install folders. It never reads their accounts, files or data.

## Children

TickPuff does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published here with a new date.
