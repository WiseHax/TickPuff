## Description

TickPuff turns your screen into a cozy little world with a big, beautiful clock.

Pick a world — a misty forest, a cherry-blossom garden, a deep aquarium, a neon city rooftop or a candle-lit
library — and it changes with the time of day: sunrise, sunset, stars and moonlight. A 3D companion lives
there with you. It wanders around, visits its favourite spots, naps at night, shelters from the rain, greets
you when you come back and even dances when your music is playing. Click it to pet it.

It's a clock first, not a dashboard. When you want them, small tools slide in from the side and slide away
again:

• Focus timer with breaks, a daily streak and a gentle chime
• Tasks, notes, a countdown and a calendar
• Weather, system monitor and now playing
• Mini mode: a tiny always-on-top clock in the corner of your screen

Calm and light on your computer: three performance modes, and everything pauses when the window is hidden.
No account, no ads, no tracking — your settings and notes stay on your computer.

TickPuff is free and open source (MIT): github.com/WiseHax/TickPuff

## What's new in this version

## Certification notes (runFullTrust)

TickPuff is a desktop app built with Tauri (Rust + WebView2), not a UWP app. runFullTrust is required to run the
desktop executable. It is used for: a frameless window with an always-on-top mini mode, a system tray icon,
reading the current Windows media session to show "Now playing", and reading local CPU/memory/GPU usage for an
optional widget. It does not access user files, run other programs, or require admin rights.
